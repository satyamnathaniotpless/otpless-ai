# AI Automation Engineer

## Posting

- **Role:** AI Automation Engineer
- **Posting link:** OTPLESS careers page, role #7
- **Comp band:** ₹20–45L (level-dependent) + ESOPs
- **Owner:** CTO

## Outreach hook patterns

- Reference a specific automation they shipped to real users: "saw you automated {workflow} — that's exactly the artifact-first bar for this role."
- For build-in-public candidates: mention their GitHub/X/hackathon history as the hook.
- For candidates who automated their own job: lead with that directly — it's the strongest possible signal for this role.

## Candidate bar

**Hard requirements**
- Full-stack scrappy: APIs, webhooks, small services, quick UIs — whatever ships.
- Hands-on with LLM APIs and agent patterns; rigorous about prompts, evals, failure modes.

**Strong signals**
- Portfolio of shipped automations — judged by artifacts, not years of experience.
- Built own product / OSS contributor / identity-auth depth (universal preference, all roles).

**Auto-advance signals**
- Strong Q1 artifact — a real shipped automation with users, not a demo.
- Strong Q4 identity/auth/fraud depth answer (e.g. automated a KYC/fraud-adjacent workflow).

**Auto-reject signals**
- No shipped-automation portfolio, only coursework/tutorial projects.
- Generic AI-written answers with no specifics about a real automation or its failure modes.

## Work-sample brief

Build a working automation for one of three internal OTPLESS workflows (support deflection, lead enrichment, or QA/test generation — pick one) in 3 hours. Judged on whether it actually runs against real inputs, plus the eval approach used to trust it. AI tools required.

## Role-specific Q&A

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Do I pick the workflow or is it assigned? | Candidate picks from the 3 options in the brief. |
| Is this judged on polish? | No — judged on whether it works and how you evaluated trust in it. |

## Evaluation emphasis

Working artifact plus rigor of eval design over polish or scope. Reward candidates who show how they'd know the automation is trustworthy, not just that it runs once.
