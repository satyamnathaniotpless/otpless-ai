# Founding Recruiter (Recruiting + People Ops)

## Posting

- **Role:** Founding Recruiter
- **Posting link:** OTPLESS careers page, role #1
- **Comp band:** ₹15–25L + ESOPs
- **Owner:** Satyam (Founder)

## Outreach hook patterns

- Reference a specific search they ran solo, intake to close — not just "recruiting experience."
- If they closed a backend/mobile/ML engineer personally, name that as the hook: "saw you closed a {role} hire end-to-end at {company}."
- For TA-builder types: "you built a TA function from zero at {company} — that's exactly the shape of this job."

## Candidate bar

**Hard requirements**
- 3–6 years full-cycle tech recruiting at product startups.
- Has personally closed backend, mobile, or ML engineers (not just coordinated).

**Strong signals**
- Pipeline-building is sourcing-heavy, not coordination-heavy.
- Has run hiring solo: intake to close, negotiation included.
- Built own product / OSS contributor / worked deeply on identity, auth, or fraud (universal preference, all roles).

**Auto-advance signals**
- Strong Q1 artifact (a hiring process, dashboard, or pipeline they built and can show).
- Strong Q4 identity/auth/fraud depth answer.

**Auto-reject signals**
- Coordination-only background, no full-cycle closes.
- No evidence of solo ownership of a search.

## Work-sample brief

Live sourcing simulation for the Security Engineer JD: given the posting and candidate bar, source and pitch 3 plausible candidate profiles in 2–3 hours, with outreach drafts and a one-line rationale for each. Judged on sourcing instinct and message quality, not volume.

## Role-specific Q&A

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Will I run all 6 searches at once? | Yes — Founding Recruiter owns simultaneous pipelines from day one. |
| Do I do people ops too? | Yes — offer paperwork, BGV, payroll coordination, onboarding, as the team grows. |

## Evaluation emphasis

Sourcing instinct and closing ability over process/coordination polish. Look for evidence they've run a search zero-to-hire alone, including the negotiation.
